# Privacy Policy — SchoolTime Pro

**Effective Date:** June 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 5, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **SchoolTime Pro** ("the App", "we", "our"). SchoolTime Pro is a school timetable management application designed to help educational institutions manage teachers, classes, subjects, and academic schedules efficiently.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using SchoolTime Pro, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When using SchoolTime Pro, you may provide the following information:

- **School Information:** School name, academic year, and period configuration settings.
- **Teacher Information:** Teacher names and subject assignments entered by the administrator.
- **Class & Subject Data:** Class names, subject names, and periods-per-week settings.
- **Timetable Data:** Period assignments, substitute entries, and scheduling configurations.
- **Login Credentials:** Email address and password (if using email/password login), or Google account information (if using Google Sign-In).

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and app version (used for crash diagnostics only).
- **App Usage Data:** Basic usage patterns to improve app performance (no personally identifiable information).

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect student personal information.
- We do **not** collect financial or payment information.
- We do **not** access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.
- We do **not** track your activity outside of the App.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

- **Provide core app functionality:** Generate timetables, manage teacher and class data, and handle schedule conflicts.
- **Authentication:** Verify user identity through email/password or Google Sign-In to distinguish Admin, HOD, and Teacher roles.
- **Backup & Restore:** Allow you to back up your school data and restore it when needed.
- **PDF Export:** Generate timetable PDFs from your school data stored on your device.
- **Improve the App:** Diagnose bugs and crashes using anonymized device diagnostics.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, marketing, or selling to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Storage

### 4.1 Local Storage

All school data — including teacher records, class records, subjects, and timetable entries — is stored **locally on your device** using an encrypted SQLite database (via the `sqflite` library). This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly use the Backup feature.

### 4.2 Cloud Authentication

If you sign in using **Google Sign-In** or email/password, your authentication credentials are managed by **Firebase Authentication** (Google LLC). Firebase stores only your email address and a hashed identifier for login purposes. Firebase's privacy policy is available at: [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### 4.3 Backup Data

When you use the Backup & Restore feature, your data is saved to a file on your device's local storage or a location you choose. We do not automatically upload backup files to any external server.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) for user login management.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or a valid legal process, we may disclose information to comply with applicable regulations.
- **Safety:** To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

---

## 6. User Roles & Access Control

SchoolTime Pro uses a role-based access system:

- **Admin:** Full access to all features including school setup, teacher management, timetable generation, backup, and settings.
- **HOD (Head of Department):** Limited access as defined by the Admin.
- **Teacher:** View-only access to timetable data. Cannot modify school settings, teacher records, or configurations.

Role assignments are managed entirely within the App by the Admin and are stored locally on the device.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

SchoolTime Pro is intended for use by **school administrators and teachers only**. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not store any student personal data.

If you believe any information from a minor has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately at the email below.

---

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

- Local data is stored in a private app database inaccessible to other apps on your device.
- Authentication is handled by Firebase with industry-standard encryption.
- We recommend using device-level screen locks and secure passwords.

However, no method of data storage or transmission is 100% secure. We encourage users to keep their devices secure and to log out of the App when not in use.

---

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the data stored about you within the App.
- **Delete** all app data by uninstalling the App or using the in-app data reset option.
- **Export** your data using the PDF Export or Backup features.
- **Revoke** Google Sign-In access through your Google account settings at [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## 10. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device is retained until you:

- Manually delete it within the App.
- Uninstall the App (which removes all local data).
- Perform a factory reset on your device.

Firebase authentication data is retained until you delete your account or revoke access.
